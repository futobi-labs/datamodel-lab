What the picture argues

  • Grain first. The banner is the most important thing on the page. If you can't say the grain in one sentence, the fact isn't ready.
  • One date table, many roles. Sales relates to Date twice. Only one relationship can be active; the ship-date measure turns the other on with USERELATIONSHIP. The alternative, a second date table, is fine when users need both dates on the same axis at once.
  • Coarser facts are honest about it. Budget is month by category by region, so it reaches Product on category through a many-to-many relationship. The dotted line is a warning label, not a mistake.
  • Conformed dimensions glow in the constellation view: Date, Product and Warehouse serve more than one fact, which is what lets a report put sales and inventory side by side.
