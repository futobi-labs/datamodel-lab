What to look for

  • Fan-in at silver. Two bronze order tables collapse into one silver.sales_order_line. That join is the single most important piece of logic in the model, so it belongs in exactly one place.
  • Fan-out at gold. One silver entity feeds a dimension and a fact. Surrogate-key lookups are why: fact_sales pulls a little from every dimension's source.
  • The orphan. dim_date has no upstream at all; it is generated. Every model has a few of these and lineage tools tend to hide them.
  • The narrowing at the semantic layer. Most measures read one or two columns. If a measure pulls from ten, that is usually logic that wants to live in gold.

The diagram is drawn from the sample model's metadata, so pointing it at a real model is a matter of replacing one JSON file.
