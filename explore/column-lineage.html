Why column level

Table lineage tells you that fact_sales depends on silver.sales_order_line. Column lineage tells you that Margin % depends on erp.sales_order_line.discount_pct and erp.product.standard_cost, and on nothing from the order header. That is the question people actually ask when a number looks wrong, and it is the question impact analysis needs answered before a source column changes.

Reading the trace

  • Edges carry a transform label: pass-through, rename, cast, lookup, derive, DAX. Hover an edge to see it. Long chains of pass-through are healthy; a derive in bronze is a smell.
  • Surrogate keys show their parentage honestly: fact_sales.customer_key traces to both the customer id and the order date, because a Type 2 lookup is a point-in-time join.
  • Measures trace through other measures. YoY % reaches the source through Net Sales PY and Net Sales.
