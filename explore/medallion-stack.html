The lakehouse as a zoomable stack. Each band is a layer, each block a table, each sliver a column, sized by column count. Click to drill in; click the left band to climb back out.

Click a block

Layers zoom to their tables, tables zoom to their columns. The panel shows what each layer owns and what a table's grain is.

Reading the stack

The widths tell a story you can check against your own platform. Bronze is wide and shallow: many tables, source-shaped, each carrying its two audit columns. Silver is narrower, because the order header and lines became one entity. Gold widens again with surrogate keys and SCD columns, but the number of tables drops to the handful a person can hold in their head. If your gold layer has more tables than your silver layer, it is worth asking which of them are really reports.
