Data Model Lab

The shape of the data, not the data.

A working notebook on how analytics models are built today: medallion layers, star schemas, semantic models and the engines underneath. Half field guide, half playground for seeing a model's structure from source system to report visual.

Read the field guide Open the explorations

Principles in one breath

  • Land it raw, once.Bronze keeps the source's shape and adds only audit columns. Shortcuts and views count.
  • Clean it where it's shared.Silver owns typing, de-duplication and the joins everyone would otherwise repeat.
  • Model to a declared grain.Gold is a star: facts at one stated grain, conformed dimensions, surrogate keys.
  • Measures, not columns.The semantic model adds relationships, meaning and DAX, and hides the plumbing.
  • Reports ask; they don't compute.If a visual needs logic, that logic belongs one layer down.
